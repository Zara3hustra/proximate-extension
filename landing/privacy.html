Privacy Policy — Proximate

Last updated: 2026-06-07

Proximate is a browser extension that selectively routes user-chosen websites through a proxy server provided and controlled by the user. This policy explains what data the extension handles.

Data we collect

None. Proximate does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. We operate no servers that receive your data.

Data stored locally

All settings are stored only in your browser via chrome.storage.local:

  • your proxy configuration (host, port, and credentials you enter);
  • the services and custom domains you choose to route.

This data never leaves your device except as network traffic to the proxy server you configured. Uninstalling the extension removes it.

Your traffic

For domains you select, requests are routed to your proxy server via a locally generated PAC script. All other traffic goes directly. We have no access to, and never see, your browsing or traffic.

Permissions

  • proxy, webRequest, webRequestAuthProvider — apply proxy routing and supply credentials to authenticated proxies.
  • storage — save your settings locally.
  • tabs — read the active tab URL for status / test actions.
  • privacy — block non-proxied WebRTC UDP to prevent real-IP leaks while active.
  • favicon — show favicons of added domains from the local browser cache (no third-party requests).
  • <all_urls> — required so the PAC can decide per request whether to proxy or go direct. The extension does not read or modify page content.

Third parties

No analytics, no trackers, no third-party data sharing. Favicons are loaded from the browser's own local cache.
